    Landscape

    The gateways route.
    The guardrails inspect.
    Neither counts.

    Tools that sit between a coding agent and a model, surveyed 2026-10-19. A tool optimised for a different job is not being criticised — and three of them are better than this project at something named below.

    Three clusters, and an empty corner

    Left — the gateways

    LiteLLM, Kong, Portkey. One OpenAI-shaped surface over a hundred providers, with keys, budgets, retries and observability — done very well. What they do not do is look at what the agent is reading or about to run: a tool result full of secrets and a rm -rf ~ pass through as ordinary bytes.

    Right and below — the rule engines

    NeMo Guardrails, Guardrails AI, LLM Guard. They inspect content properly, and several do it better than this project. What they are not is a gateway: they are libraries you call, so an agent that does not call them is unprotected, and swapping harness loses them entirely.

    Top right — empty except this

    Nobody else combines the gateway's position — every request, every client, no opt-out — with the rule engine's inspection and a budget the server counts. That is a claim about coverage, not about quality.

    Where this project is behind

    Worth stating, since a landscape where one tool wins every column is a landscape nobody believes.

    Provider breadth — LiteLLM, by a wide margin
    A hundred providers against whatever you configure here. If the question is "call many providers through one client", LiteLLM is simply the answer — and ai-bunker can sit in front of it rather than compete.
    Content classifiers — LLM Guard and Guardrails AI
    Their scanners are trained models and curated validator hubs; this ships versioned regular expressions with an entropy gate. Reviewable, diffable and free to run — a deliberate trade, and on raw detection quality it is a trade, not a win.
    Policy expressiveness and testing — OPA
    Rego expresses conditions a regex cannot, and OPA's test tooling with coverage is better than rules --check. The idea of policy carrying its own tests was taken from there.
    Maturity
    This is 0.1.0-alpha next to projects with years of production use.

    Ideas taken from the field

    None of this was invented here, and saying so costs nothing.

    • Open Policy Agent

      Policy carries its own tests, and decisions are logged from day one. Both are now load-bearing here — and the first caught three wrong patterns before they reached a deployment.

    • gitleaks

      Regex and an entropy gate, plus per-rule allowlists. A scanner that cries wolf teaches its readers to ignore it.

    • Microsoft's spotlighting

      Mark retrieved content as data. Pattern matching catches clumsy injection attempts; the structural marking is what holds when the patterns miss.

    • Agent-harness research

      The "prevention versus cure" split: bound context structurally, or let it grow and compress. This project is prevention, moved server-side — which the taxonomy does not have a box for.
