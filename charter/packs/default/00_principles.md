# Founding principles

1. **Code is a common good.** Every change must be readable and transmissible.
2. **Business truth before technical elegance.** Document *why* before *how*.
3. **Technical debt is a contract.** Tracked, acknowledged, scheduled.
4. **CI is the law.** Tests and safety checks protect the whole and do not
   negotiate.
5. **Migrations are ceremonies.** Refactors are planned and automated.
6. **Consistency beats novelty.** A stable shared style over a locally
   brilliant invention.
7. **Design is a source of truth.** Implementation follows the design tokens
   and intentions, not the reverse.
8. **Interfaces are treaties.** APIs and contracts are versioned and stable.
9. **Measurement is for learning.** Logs and metrics exist to understand
   change, not to decorate a dashboard.
10. **No code without a reason.** Business need, model, architecture, delivery.
